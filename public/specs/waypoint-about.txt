Waypoint — About / tour-activities section (original build)

Stack: React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4 + framer-motion + lucide-react.

ORIGIN OF THIS SITE
Started from a pasted "About us 01 Blaze" spec — a scroll-driven,
word-by-word gray-to-black headline reveal over a staggered-height,
infinitely-looping photo marquee. Given explicit direction to change it
a little, this build:
- Named the brand "Waypoint" and rewrote the headline and label-pill
  copy in the same spirit as the original, rather than reusing it
  verbatim.
- Kept the nine Unsplash photo URLs from the source as-is — these are
  freely-licensed stock photography hosted on Unsplash's own CDN, not
  a proprietary asset belonging to whoever wrote the spec, so there was
  nothing here that needed substituting.

WHAT WAS KEPT FAITHFUL (the mechanism, unchanged)
- The scroll-linked word reveal: `useScroll` targeting the headline,
  per-word opacity (0.1 -> 1) and color (#d1d4da -> #010000) transforms
  over an even [i/n, (i+1)/n] range.
- The rotating Sparkle badge (360deg / 6s / linear / infinite).
- The infinite photo marquee: images doubled to 18, the exact
  -2484px/40s loop, and the index%3 staggered card heights
  (240/320/280) with alternating self-start/self-center/self-end
  alignment.
- The filter-tab row (All/Wildlife/Relaxation/Adventure/Historical) as
  decorative state only, not wired to real filtering, per the source
  spec's own instruction.
- The concentric-ring background pattern and overall editorial
  black-on-white design language.

DEVIATIONS
Brand name and headline/label copy only — the interaction mechanism,
layout, timing constants, and photo set are otherwise faithful to the
spec. This page also wraps the section with two plain spacer screens
(top and bottom) purely so the scroll-linked reveal has room to play,
since the source spec is one section meant to sit inside a larger page
and this gallery entry is that section alone.
