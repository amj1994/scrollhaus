Aurum — "Sculpted By Time" luxury jewelry scroll experience (original build)

Stack: React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4 + framer-motion + lucide-react.

ORIGIN OF THIS SITE
Started from a pasted "SCULPTED BY TIME" spec: a 650vh scroll-driven
cinematic jewelry landing page — two background videos scrubbed by scroll
position, editorial headline/copy that fades-blurs-and-lifts away as you
scroll, and two product panels that slide in from the left at specific
scroll thresholds. The spec named five exact Cloudinary-hosted asset URLs
(two background videos, three product images) belonging to whoever wrote
it. Per explicit direction to treat the spec as inspiration and generate
fresh imagery, this build:
- Generated two original background videos via Higgsfield (a macro shot
  of molten gold swirling in black space for the first 40% of scroll,
  and a rotating faceted black-onyx gemstone for the back half) in place
  of the source's hosted MP4s.
- Generated three original product photographs via Higgsfield (the hero
  ring, background-removed to a true transparent PNG for the "object
  contain" hero card; the pink-sapphire earrings; the sculptural silver
  ring) in place of the source's hosted JPGs.
- Named the brand "Aurum" (added a small wordmark next to the crest —
  the source spec's header had an icon only, no name).

WHAT WAS KEPT FAITHFUL (the mechanism, unchanged)
- The double-lerp scroll-scrubbing engine: scroll position smoothed at
  0.05, each video's currentTime smoothed at 0.08 toward a target ratio
  derived from scroll phase, gated by `!video.seeking` on every seek so
  the decoder never gets flooded.
- The exact phase math: video 1 scrubs 0 -> 1 across scroll 0-40%, holds
  at the first product panel (40-50%), video 2 scrubs across 50-90%, then
  holds at the second panel past 90%.
- The staggered fade/blur/lift-away treatment on every editorial element
  (logo, subheader, nav, three headline lines, two manifesto paragraphs,
  right-column caption/card/price), each on its own [start, end] window
  mapped from scroll progress to opacity, translateY and blur.
- The slide-in product panel: same open windows, same "BACK TO SERIES"
  behavior (scrolls to 35% or 82% depending on which panel), same
  quantity stepper and dynamic per-product pricing, same product-swap
  logic (Aura earrings vs. the sculpture ring) tied to scroll position.
- The bag icon jumping to the very end of the page; the top progress bar;
  the color system (#020202 background, #FBFF8D accent, #FAF9F5 panel).
- The frame-sequence canvas/JPG fallback described in the source spec
  was explicitly noted there as unused dead code (the live page only
  ever scrubs the two MP4s) — this build omits it rather than building
  an inert fallback with no real assets behind it.

DEVIATIONS
Brand wordmark added; all five media assets are original generations,
not the spec author's hosted files.
